# Visual Mathematical Dictionary

A reference where every mathematical concept is explained *visually first*: the
picture carries the meaning, and the words and notation follow it.

## Why

Most mathematical definitions are written for people who already understand
them. A learner meets "a derivative is the limit of the difference quotient"
and gets a sentence, not a mental image. This project inverts that: each entry
leads with a diagram or animation that makes the idea obvious, and only then
gives the formal statement so the reader can connect the two.

## What an entry contains

Each concept is one self-contained entry with:

- **Visual** — a diagram or interactive animation that shows the idea in motion.
- **Plain-language meaning** — what it says, with no notation.
- **Formal definition** — the standard statement and notation.
- **Worked intuition** — why the formal statement matches the picture.
- **Links** — prerequisite concepts and concepts that build on this one.

Entries connect to each other, so the dictionary reads as a graph of ideas
rather than a flat alphabetical list.
