Reading the radar: the centre is you. For flights, angle = compass bearing and distance from centre = how far away (outer ring = your range). For satellites, it's a sky-dome: centre = straight up (zenith), the rim = the horizon, angle = compass azimuth.

Map view: your location is the marker, the circle is your flight range (the area the radar covers), and each plane sits at its real position. Click a plane for details.
